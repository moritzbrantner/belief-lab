2. Record a source-backed position

Select a prepared evidence record with a source-local person ID. Enter the exact passage and its offsets in the original source. This records an analyst annotation, not a source-verified extraction. Leave any score empty when evidence is insufficient.

Explicit suspension needs the person's own (or reported) words withholding a position; hesitation or silence is not suspension. Dates come from the source: when the person said it is distinct from when it was published or found.

Recording new evidence invalidates any previous person-confidence assessment for that source-local person until explicitly reassessed.
