Why the number is safe to read

Scores carry their meaning.

  • detection_confidence describes a detector output.
  • ner_confidence describes an NLP extraction.
  • entity_link_probability describes an identity hypothesis.
  • heuristic_strength describes this baseline rule.

None are silently relabeled as Bayesian posterior probability.
