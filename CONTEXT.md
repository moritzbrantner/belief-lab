# Belief Lab

Source-grounded attribution of people's expressed or held beliefs, preserving the
distinction between source evidence, interpretation and proposition truth.

## Language

**Source text**: Text extracted or normalized from a source document by its owning
producer. It is source evidence, distinct from speech transcripts, OCR observations,
extracted proposition candidates and an investigator's own assertions.

**Proposition candidate**: A reviewable interpretation of what source material
expresses. Its extraction confidence does not establish a person's endorsement,
conviction or the proposition's truth.
